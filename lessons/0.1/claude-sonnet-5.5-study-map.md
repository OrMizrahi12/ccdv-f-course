# CCDV-F Study Map (from the exam guide only)

## Exam basics
- **Items:** 53
- **Item types:** multiple-choice and multiple-response (each item states how many answers to select)
- **Time limit:** 120 minutes (about 2.3 min/item, my estimate)
- **Passing score:** scaled 720 on a 100-1,000 scale
- **Fee and validity:** $125, valid 12 months

## Domains, heaviest first
Item counts are **estimates** (weight × 53, rounded to 1 decimal).

The domain weights sum to 100%, and each domain's skill weights sum to its domain weight.

## What the guide doesn't say
- Whether all 53 items are scored. The guide describes weights as the share of *scored* items, so the real scored count may be lower.
- How many items are multiple-choice versus multiple-response.
- Whether multiple-response items give partial credit, or whether guessing is penalized.
- How many items you must answer correctly to reach 720. The scaled score is not mapped to a percent-correct.
- How items are distributed within a domain beyond the skill weights above.

**Note:** Claude Code and Eval/Debugging together are only about 5.7% of the exam. Applications and Integration alone is about a third, so the weights favor spending your time there first.
